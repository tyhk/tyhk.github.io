G7網路安全工作小組在多國機關（含CISA、BSI、ANSSI、ACN、CSE、NCSC、Japan NCO 與 EU Commission）協作下發布《Software Bill of Materials for AI – Minimum Elements》，提出 AI 專用 SBOM（亦稱 AIBOM）的自願性最低要素。文件以七大「clusters」（Metadata、Models、Dataset Properties、System Level Properties、Key Performance Indicators、Security Properties、Infrastructure）為架構，將模型、資料集屬性、系統與安全屬性等納入，目標是提升 AI 供應鏈透明度與可追溯性，並強調須結合弱點掃描、通報等工具，SBOM 本身不足以確保安全。 [來源] CISA, G7 partners release SBOM for AI guidance to boost AI supply chain transparency and cybersecurity resilience: https://industrialcyber.co/sbom/cisa-g7-partners-release-sbom-for-ai-guidance-to-boost-ai-supply-chain-transparency-and-cybersecurity-resilience/ [來源] Global Cyber Agencies Issue New SBOMs for AI Guidance to Tackle AI Supply Chain Risks: https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/new-sboms-for-ai-guidance-2026/ [來源] Major world economies spell out key elements of AI 'ingredients lists': https://cyberscoop.com/g7-cisa-ai-sbom-security-guidance/ [來源] CISA's AI SBOM guidance pushes software supply-chain oversight into new territory: https://www.csoonline.com/article/4170694/cisas-ai-sbom-guidance-pushes-software-supply-chain-oversight-into-new-territory.html [來源] AI SBOM Minimum Elements: CISA-G7 Baseline for Supply Chain Transparency: https://windowsforum.com/threads/ai-sbom-minimum-elements-cisa-g7-baseline-for-supply-chain-transparency.417650/
