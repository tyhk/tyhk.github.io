萊特州立大學網路防禦卓越資格延至2031

🟢 Low Research & Best Practices

最可靠來源是 Wright State University 新聞稿，指出該校與其 Master of Science in Cyber Security（網路安全碩士）計畫的 National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense（CAE-CD）認證已延長至 2031 年，且該資格自 2020 年首次取得。其次可參考 NSA 的 National Centers of Academic Excellence in Cybersecurity（NCAE-C）官方頁面，了解 CAE-CD 的官方制度與授權背景。 [來源] Wright State's designation as National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense extended through 2031 | Wright State University: https://webapp2.wright.edu/web1/newsroom/2026/05/13/wright-states-designation-as-national-center-of-academic-excellence-in-cyber-defense-extended-through-2031/ [來源] National Centers of Academic Excellence in Cybersecurity | National Security Agency (NSA): https://www.nsa.gov/Academics/Centers-of-Academic-Excellence/ [來源] Wright State's designation as National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense extended through 2031 (MS Cyber Security page) | Wright State University: https://webapp2.wright.edu/web1/newsroom/2026/05/13/wright-states-designation-as-national-center-of-academic-excellence-in-cyber-defense-extended-through-2031/cyber-security-ms-national-center-of-academic-excellence-cecs/

📊 影響分析

  • 受影響行業: Education, Government, Critical Infrastructure
  • 直接影響金融機構: 否
  • 影響中港澳台: 否
  • 風險評級: Low

🎯 CISO 應對行動

  • 與萊特州立大學建立人才合作夥伴關係，利用其卓越的網路防禦教育資源招聘和培訓資安專業人員
  • 關注其課程與認證是否符合組織需求，並將其列為人才儲備渠道之一
  • 定期評估學術機構的研究成果，汲取最佳實踐以提升組織資安成熟度。
